#+TITLE: DamageBDD Australian Crypto Tax Evidence (prototype)
#+DESCRIPTION: Read-only CLN/NWC/on-chain evidence, historic AUD values, audit pack and æternity hash anchoring

* Status and compliance boundary

This package is an ADDITIVE, REVIEW-REQUIRED PROTOTYPE for an Australian
crypto record-keeping workflow. It is NOT ATO certified, cannot lodge a BAS or
tax return, and is not an automatic assessment of taxable income, CGT or GST.
A registered tax agent / qualified reviewer must confirm decisions, the
entity's accounting method and the completeness of all asset inventories.

The report is "attestable" only when every imported non-allocation event has
an archived price witness, an archived source snapshot and an explicit approved
classification. "Attestable" means internally complete for *the imported
records*, not proof that all transactions were imported or that tax advice is
correct. On-chain signatures certify publication of a digest, not ATO approval.

* Source integration

- =damage_cln:list_all_invoices(#{page_limit => 200, order => asc})=: complete
  historic invoice listing, with pagination performed by the existing CLN helper.
  Only invoices with =status=paid= and actual =paid_at= / =amount_received_msat=
  become receipt evidence. Creation alone is NOT revenue.
  =damage_tax:ingest_cln/1= fails closed unless application config
  =tax_cln_owner= exactly matches the requested legal-entity/account identifier.
  A shared node needs an additional per-business attribution adapter; do not
  reuse the global CLN importer for arbitrary customer wallet accounts.
- =damage_cln:list_pays/0=: only completed outgoing payments are imported;
  principal and routing fee are separate events. Failed/pending attempts are
  ignored. Outgoing does NOT automatically mean a tax deduction.
- =damage_tax:import_chain/2=: caller-supplied *confirmed* Bitcoin/æternity
  transaction evidence with explicit asset units/decimals, confirmed timestamp,
  block hash, positive block height, positive confirmations, transaction ID
  and direction. Importing a funding or closing transaction does not
  automatically make it taxable.
- =damage_tax:import_nwc/2=: a client-scoped ledger movement is an internal
  allocation. It never creates a second receipt for the same Bitcoin payment.
  A separately observed CLN payment is the payment source of record.

=damage_tax_cln= does not currently ingest CLN =listforwards= routing-income
history, on-chain =listtransactions=, node channel balance snapshots, mining or
staking income, fiat exchange trades, or L402 business invoice receivables.
Import independent confirmed records through =import_chain= or a future
adapter before claiming year-wide completeness. The chain importer requires a
transaction identifier, block hash, positive block height, confirmation count
and confirmed timestamp but DOES NOT independently verify Bitcoin Merkle proofs
or æternity inclusion. Supply them from a trusted node/indexer and reconcile.
Inclusion, ownership and confirmation depth need separate verification.
Incoming invoices paid to the node may belong to DIFFERENT customers; scope
per legal entity rather than blindly allocating the entire node to every
wallet owner.

* Historical AUD pricing

=damage_tax_prices:fetch(cryptocompare, Asset, UnixSeconds)= retrieves
CryptoCompare =/data/v2/histohour= for BTC, ETH or AE to AUD. The applied rate
is the =open= of the UTC hour containing the event. It cannot include later
trades in that hour. This is a reasonable historical market proxy, not an
exchange-specific executed price; for material amounts prefer transaction-
specific exchange statements and retain them as separate evidence.

=damage_tax_prices:fetch(coingecko, Asset, UnixSeconds)= is an optional daily
historical fallback. It requires historical API access (Demo tier typically
covers only the most recent 365 days). Its daily granularity triggers
=coarse_price_requires_approval= until the reviewer explicitly approves it.
Keys are optional headers from =tax_cryptocompare_key= and
=tax_coingecko_demo_key= application settings; they are not included in
canonical price evidence. Public endpoints, rate limits, access and historical
availability can change and have NOT been live-tested in this release.

Every price quote has a provider, method, UTC timestamp, AUD fixed-point rate,
resolution, response SHA-256 and retrieval timestamp. Store the exact raw HTTP
response in the PRIVATE evidence directory before approving a price. Never
silently substitute an unsupported token price or today's exchange rate.

* Accounting decisions

Each source event begins unclassified. Decisions are provided independently in
an event-ID keyed map. Permitted categories:

- =ordinary_income=: candidate business receipt amount in AUD.
- =business_expense=: candidate business expenditure; deductibility unassessed.
- =cgt_disposal=: a disposal with reviewer-supplied acquisition timestamp,
  cost base in AUD cents and independent lot reference. Only UNDISCOUNTED
  gains and losses are computed; discounts, exemptions and loss-order rules are
  NOT computed.
- =cost_basis_acquisition=: acquisition record and candidate cost basis;
  not automatically a deduction or gain.
- =internal_transfer=: a documented move between accounts/wallets under the
  same beneficial ownership (review and price witness still required).
- =non_tax_event=: reviewed non-tax movement.
- =review=: the mandatory default for unclassified events.

NWC allocations are flagged =non_tax_event= by default because they are not
independent blockchain/CLN asset receipts, but the underlying real payment
must still be reconciled. Outgoing payments cannot silently be classified as income and incoming
receipts cannot silently be classified as deductible business expenses; a
conflicting classification blocks attestation pending an adjustment workflow.
Fees are not automatically deductible. Account for
business-as-trading-stock vs investor-on-capital-account distinctions and
cash-basis vs accruals-basis recognition separately. If income is recognised
on an invoice date under the accounting method, settled CLN receipt evidence
alone is not enough to determine the tax period of assessable income.

GST is not calculated. In a GST-registered enterprise, receiving qualifying
digital currency as payment for a taxable supply generally follows the
underlying supply's GST treatment; GST on a BAS must be expressed in AUD.
Digital currency itself may have a different GST treatment to the supply.
Keep GST tax invoices/credit notes with business source documents and review
input tax credits separately.

FY is 1 July to the following 30 June. =fy_bounds/2= accepts the financial
YEAR OF END and a configured local standard UTC offset in minutes for the
1 July boundaries: Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane +600, Adelaide +570,
Darwin +570, Perth +480. July boundaries occur during standard time.
All transaction timestamps are UTC Unix seconds.

* Files and setup

Files under the monorepo root:

: apps/damage/src/damage_tax*.erl
: apps/damage/src/steps_tax.erl
: apps/damage/test/damage_tax_tests.erl
: contracts/tax_evidence_anchor.aes
: features/tax/ato_cln_provenance.feature
: docs/tax/README.org

Start the store ONCE via =damage_sup= under an explicit opt-in configuration.
For example, add a worker child after =damage_cln= is initialized:

#+begin_src erlang
#{id => damage_tax_store,
  start => {damage_tax, start_link, ["/var/lib/damage/tax/journal.jsonl"]},
  restart => permanent, shutdown => 5000, type => worker,
  modules => [damage_tax_store]}
#+end_src

Register =steps_tax= with the configured DamageBDD step modules to execute
=features/tax/ato_cln_provenance.feature=. The uploaded source does not include
the currently deployed =damage_sup.erl= / =damage_config.erl=, so startup
registration and BDD module registration must be applied against that version
rather than guessed here. EUnit tests can run without enabling the BDD module.

Choose a dedicated path owned by the daemon account. The journal is mode 0600,
and =evidence/= is 0700 with per-file 0600, but this is *not encryption*.
Protect the entire parent directory, backups and operator account. The journal
is a single-writer store, intended for one DamageBDD instance per journal;
DO NOT mount it as a concurrent multi-node append log. Replicate using trusted
backups / authenticated event distribution and merge in a separate process.

Optional application settings are simple tuples:

#+begin_src erlang
{damage, [
  {tax_cln_owner, <<"ak_REPLACE_WITH_CLN_OPERATOR_ACCOUNT">>},
  {tax_cryptocompare_key, <<"FROM_SECRETS_STORE">>},
  {tax_coingecko_demo_key, <<"FROM_SECRETS_STORE">>}
]}
#+end_src

Do not put real API keys into =sys.config= checked into Git. Inject them via a
secret manager. Credentials used for tax sources MUST NOT be recorded in a
report or written on chain.

* Example Erlang shell workflow

#+begin_src erlang
Tenant = <<"ak_REPLACE_WITH_REAL_ACCOUNT">>.
{ok, Imported} = damage_tax:ingest_cln(Tenant).
{ok, Events} = damage_tax:records(Tenant).

Needs = damage_tax_report:required_prices(Events, 2026, 600).
Quotes = lists:foldl(fun({Asset, Hour}, Acc) ->
    {ok, Quote} = damage_tax:fetch_and_archive_quote(cryptocompare, Asset, Hour),
    Acc#{{Asset, Hour} => Quote}
end, #{}, Needs).

%% The reviewer must supply an explicit decision for each event.
%% Example ONLY: ordinary_income is not valid for every incoming payment.
Decisions = #{
  %% <<"event-id-sha256">> => #{<<"category">> => <<"ordinary_income">>,
  %%      <<"approved">> => true, <<"reviewer">> => <<"reviewer-id">>}
}.

{ok, Report} = damage_tax_report:build(2026, 600, Events, Quotes, Decisions).
Review = maps:get(<<"review_required">>, maps:get(<<"manifest">>, Report)).
ok = damage_tax_report:export_private(Report, "/var/lib/damage/tax/FY2026").

%% Only if all classifications are approved and evidence is archived:
{ok, Proof} = damage_tax_attest:prepare(Report).
%% Submission signs with an explicit authorised æternity account keypair:
%% {ok, Submitted} = damage_tax_attest:submit(OwnerKeyPair, ContractId, Report).
#+end_src

Evidence for a publicly fetched price is stored as raw HTTP JSON at
=evidence/<lowercase_sha256>.json=, with the SHA in the report manifest.
For native CLN responses the saved snapshot is a deliberate allowlisted view;
preimages, secrets and BOLT11 data are excluded. Preserve a separate encrypted
native CLN export through your normal regulated backup policy for source
reconstruction; hash commitments alone do not reproduce missing records.

* On-chain anchoring

The small =TaxEvidenceAnchor= Sophia contract emits:

: ReportCommitted(caller, "AU-FY-2026", manifest_sha256,
:                 evidence_set_sha256, "damage_tax_au_review_v1")

One transaction per approved report/version is sufficient. A replacement
report MUST be a new on-chain revision/commitment, never a mutation to prior
records. After submission, verify inclusion/finality independently through
an æternity node or middleware; the library returns status
=submitted_check_chain_confirmation=, not "finalised". An optional AEX-141
NFT may reference the *confirmed* transaction and public manifest hash, but
should NOT carry client names, payment hashes, tax returns, or source data.

The caller is responsible for deploying/auditing the contract and explicitly
authorising the signing account. Do not use node administrator keys for an
unreviewed tax report. On-chain attestation proves commitment and signer,
not correctness of financial records or tax assessment.

* ATO record keeping and limitations

Official Australian Taxation Office guidance (checked October 2026):

- https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/crypto-asset-investments/keeping-crypto-records
- https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/income-you-must-declare/crypto-asset-business
- https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/your-industry/financial-services-and-insurance/gst-and-digital-currency

ATO asks to retain AUD values, transaction times, receipts, counterparties and
wallet/exchange records. General crypto record retention is 5 years from the
later relevant preparation/transaction/CGT event trigger; keep longer where
necessary for cost base, amendment periods or other retention obligations.
A blockchain digest does NOT replace the underlying paper or electronic files.

Not implemented: source completeness auditing, balances and UTXO/channel
reconciliation, independent tax-agent digital signatures, GST/BAS forms,
capital account FIFO/specific lot matching, CGT discount rules, inventory /
trading-stock adjustments, FX conversion for indirect quotes, historical
provider outage fallback, auto-scheduled sync, or ATO lodgement. Integrate
and validate these before describing the product as end-to-end ATO compliant.
