* Introducing the ECAI Red Lines & Guardrails Policy
Elliptic Curve AI (ECAI) is a deterministic intelligence system designed for
verification, resilience, and peace.  
But power without boundaries is risk.  
That is why DamageBDD has published the **ECAI Red Lines & Guardrails Policy**.

This executive release defines two sets of principles:

**Red Lines (Never Cross)**
- No autonomous lethal decisions
- No non-consensual mass surveillance
- No irrevocable harm to individuals
- No automation of crime or suppression
- No “truth monopolies” that silence human appeal
- No centralized control of verification

**Guardrails (Always Enforce)**
- Human-in-the-loop on critical outcomes
- Full cryptographic auditability
- Privacy-first knowledge encoding
- Consent + revocation channels
- Threshold cryptography (no single keys)
- Staged rollouts, red-teamed and audited
- Emergency pause controls
- Transparent reporting
- Incentives aligned with safety
