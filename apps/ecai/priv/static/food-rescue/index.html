A FAIR COMPARISON

Rescue more. Show the work.

The baseline uses first-expiry-first-out and the earliest feasible arrival. The rescue planner searches all permitted whole-lot assignments in this small fixed timetable. Both use the same release checks. A stronger conventional optimiser can match the result.

What does “exact” mean here?

Six lots, six scheduled trips, fixed routes and known demand. The planner maximises eligible kilograms, then minimises kilogram-minutes to arrival, then round-trip travel time. It is not a general vehicle-routing optimum, a new AI benchmark or a forecast of real-world savings.

Late recalls are withheld from planning and introduced during execution. A plan optimal before a recall may not remain optimal afterwards; this demo traces and holds the affected stock, rather than silently re-planning with future knowledge.
