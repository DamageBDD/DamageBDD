What this demo proves — and what it does not

A fully specified small problem

One-hour slots, fixed 7 kW chargers, 14 kWh per vehicle, arrival/departure windows and a shared site limit. The exact optimiser evaluates every assignment in this discrete model. A separate checker recalculates constraints and costs.

Compare against competent alternatives

“Charge early” prioritises the earliest departure while respecting capacity. “Tariff-aware” tries the cheapest available hours, also prioritising departures. A tie with the exact optimum is reported honestly.

Synthetic, not a field trial

Prices are invented AUD tariffs, not live market or retail data. Costs exclude fixed charges, losses, taxes and grid dynamics. This is a teaching and verification demo, not an electricity bill forecast or device controller.

Execution and trust boundary

This public release runs conventional scheduling and local checks inside the ECAI dashboard. It does not call native ECAI retrieval, an LLM or the DamageBDD backend. A checksum is not a signature, a sensor attestation or proof of real-world savings.

External checker, after installing the bundle: python3 apps/ecai/tools/verify_energyflex.py ENERGYFLEX.evidence.json. Use HTTPS or localhost for browser checksum export.
