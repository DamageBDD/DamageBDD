🏛️ Core Roles of the Bitcoin Only Party (BoP)

This document defines the core operational roles within BoP in accordance with the Constitution (Sections 5 and 8). These roles are designed for sovereignty, decentralization, and immutable accountability.

Finality. Integrity. Verification. **

—

🧭 1. Coordinator – Ops & Comms Commander

Status: VACANT – Apply via Nostr Mandated by: Constitution Article 5.2

  • Oversees internal communications and national coordination
  • Manages public-facing media (website, Nostr, Matrix, IPFS, bulletins)
  • Authenticates Node identities (PGP, LNURL, Lightning proofs)
  • Publishes verified updates and ensures protocol discipline

—

🧮 2. Treasurer – Key Guardian & Flow Auditor

Status: VACANT – Apply via Nostr Mandated by: Constitution Articles 5.2 & 8

  • Manages multisig wallets (2-of-3 or 3-of-5)
  • Oversees Lightning channels and balance coordination
  • Publishes treasury reports to Nostr or IPFS
  • Signs PSBTs and verifies all expenditures publicly

—

📜 3. Policy Verifier – The Constitutional Oraclizer

Status: VACANT – Apply via Nostr Mandated by: Constitution Article 5.2

  • Validates policies and proposals via BDD or smart contract logic
  • Commits test coverage and milestone verification hashes
  • Rejects unverifiable claims or off-chain promises
  • Publishes proofs to public append-only logs

—

⚙️ 4. Tech Steward – Infrastructure and InfoSec Custodian

Status: VACANT – Apply via Nostr Mandated by: Constitution Article 5.2

  • Maintains BoP infrastructure: website, Git, Matrix, Nostr, Core nodes
  • Enforces Tor routing, reproducibility, and public key auth
  • Deploys and maintains decentralized voting infra
  • Coordinates updates to IPFS and self-hosted services

—

🕸 5. Council Node Delegate – Local Chapter Representative

Status: VACANT – Filled per region Mandated by: Constitution Article 5.1

  • Represents a geographic node in the BoP Council
  • Participates in multisig governance and PSBT quorum
  • Casts public-signed votes and verifies regional membership
  • Maintains uptime and presence in Node operations

—

🎓 6. Candidate Vetting Committee – Voter Integrity Watch

Status:

  • VACANT – Member 1
  • VACANT – Member 2
  • VACANT – Member 3

Mandated by: Constitution Article 7

  • Verifies alignment with BoP preamble
  • Audits campaign finances in satoshis
  • Ensures milestone-based promises are cryptographically committed
  • Issues endorsements via Nostr signature quorum

—

🔍 7. Verification Officer – Audit & Constitutional Integrity

Status: VACANT – Apply via Nostr Mandated by: Constitution Article 11

  • Maintains SHA-256 hash proofs of all constitutional changes
  • Tracks GPG signatures for Node roles and amendments
  • Commits state hashes to Git/IPFS
  • Publishes proofs of verifiability for all BoP decisions

—

🪙 Treasury Policy Snapshot (Article 8)

  • All treasury funds in 3-of-5 Bitcoin multisig wallets
  • No fiat accounts permitted
  • Lightning payouts require proof-of-work receipts
  • Transactions published via Nostr/IPFS for auditability

—

🗳 Election Procedure (Article 5.3)

  • Annual elections using Bitcoin-signed or PGP ballots
  • Term limits: 2 years per role
  • Results published with cryptographic proof
  • Resignations must be signed and timestamped on public record

—

🛡 Operational Integrity

All BoP core members must:

  • Avoid all fiat infrastructure
  • Operate via Lightning or on-chain Bitcoin proofs
  • Uphold verification-first governance
  • Publish decisions with public, verifiable, and timestamped transparency
